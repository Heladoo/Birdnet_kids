A visit starts with the first activity after 30 minutes of quiet. A user is a browser seen for the first time (one phone or computer, not a person; clearing site data makes it new). Active time only counts while the page is open and touched, scrolled or typed in during the last minute, in 30-second steps, so it is rounded and slightly low. Counts are anonymous numbers only.
